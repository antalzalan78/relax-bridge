Good to know.
Book an appointment.
You can easily book an appointment via the website. Choose between Studio visit or Home service, select your preferred type of massage, and pick a sutable time slot. Prefer personal contact? Feel free to send me a message via WhatsApp. Does the time no longer suit you? Feel free to send me a message to reschedule or cancel your appointment.
Prices and payment.
Payment takes place after the treatment and can easily be made by card on location. Payment by card is also possible for home service. The current price are shown during the booking process, based on the selected duration of the treatment.
Home service.
I come to your home and bring everything needed – you only need to relax. The price includes travel time, preparation, and the treatment. During booking, the system will ask for your exact address and phone number. This helps me reach you easily and ensures everything runs smoothly around your appointment. Please note: shorter treatments are not available for home service.

The Relax Massage helps you slow down, unwind, and recharge. With gentle, flowing movements, it reduces tension, improves your well-being, and supports the balance between body and mind.
The Deep Tissuemassage focuses on releasing tension and adhesions in the deeper layers of the muscles. During the treatment, I use slower, firmer, and targeted techniques that help reduce chronic muscle tension, relieve pain, and restore the natural function of the muscles. It is especially suitable for long-term muscle pain or complaints that have been present for a longer period of time.
The Neck, Shoulder & Back massage is especially recommended for desk work, stress, or prolonged physical strain, when the shoulders and back are continuously overloaded. During the treatment, I use targeted, deeper techniques that help release tight muscles, reduce pain, and improve freedom of movement.
The Facial Massage focuses on relaxing the facial muscles, refreshing the skin, and restoring its natural glow. A facial massage not only has an aesthetic effect, but also provides deep relaxation, helping you to unwind and recharge. It is especially recommended during stressful periods, in case of fatigue, or when your skin needs extra care and renewal. It can also be perfectly combined with a relaxation or back massage.
The Foot Massage focuses on refreshing and relaxing tired and heavily used feet. During the treatment, I work on different points of the sole and the foot, helping to reduce tension, improve circulation, and contribute to complete physical and mental relaxation. It is especially recommended after long periods of standing, walking, or physical strain, when the feet are overworked. A foot massage not only benefits the feet, but also supports the balance of the entire body.
