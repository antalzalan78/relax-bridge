The Foot Massage focuses on refreshing and relaxing tired and heavily used feet. During the treatment I work on different points of the sole and the foot, helping to reduce tension, improve circulation and contribute to complete physical and mental relaxation.

Especially recommended after long periods of standing, walking or physical strain, when the feet are overworked. A foot massage does not only benefit the feet, but also supports the balance of the entire body.
